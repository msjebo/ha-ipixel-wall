# Third-party notices / Derde partijen

## English
- **DMD font** (`custom_components/ipixel_wall/dmd_font.py`): the base glyphs come from
  `LCD_FONT` in [luma.core](https://github.com/rm-hull/luma.core),
  © 2017-2021 Richard Hull and contributors, MIT licence. Extended with own glyphs
  (descenders, €, °, accents).
- **iPIXEL Color**: this integration drives the panels through the
  [ha-ipixel-color](https://github.com/cagcoach/ha-ipixel-color) integration and the
  [pypixelcolor](https://pypi.org/project/pypixelcolor/) library, which must be installed separately.
- The first version (0.2.0) of `ipixel_wall` was an experimental package; everything from 0.3.0
  onwards was rewritten and extended in this project.
